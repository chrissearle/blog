Messier 106 is an intermediate spiral galaxy in the constellation Canes Venatici. It was
discovered by Pierre Méchain in 1781. M106 is at a distance of about 22 to 25 million light-years away from Earth. M106
contains an active nucleus classified as a Type 2 Seyfert, and the presence of a central supermassive black hole has
been demonstrated from radio-wavelength observations of the rotation of a disk of molecular gas orbiting within the
inner light-year around the black hole. NGC 4217 is a possible companion galaxy of Messier 106. Besides the two
visible arms, it has two "anomalous arms" detectable using an X-ray telescope.[^1]
