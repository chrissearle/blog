IC 1318 is the diffuse emission nebula and a H II region surrounding the star Sadr at the center of Cygnus's cross thus getting a nickname Sadr Region. The Sadr Region is one of the surrounding nebulous regions; others include the Butterfly Nebula and the Crescent Nebula.[^1]
